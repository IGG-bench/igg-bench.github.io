What is IGG?

IGG tests whether agents can reveal hidden targets and click them.

The task

The agent sees screenshots and its action history. It has eight actions to reveal the target and make one final click.

The benchmark

601 tasks across 16 web environments, with seven types of visibility constraints.

Results

Humans and models

Humans solve 94.6% of tasks. GPT-5.5 solves 79.4%. All tested open models remain below one third.

Revealing the target is hard

For Qwen3-VL, naming the reveal action raises accuracy from 30.8% to 33.9%. Revealing the target for it raises accuracy to 81.9%.

On 120 held-out tasks, the base models mostly click. None uses drag.

Training helps

Frequency-balanced SFT and online GRPO improve all three 8B models by 17–19 points on 120 held-out tasks. Qwen3-VL’s movable-occlusion accuracy rises from 0% to 62.5%.
